# About — Angel Ade-Oduntan

## Hi, I'm Angel.

An operations strategist and UC Berkeley Haas MBA candidate, interested in how AI is changing the way complex organizations operate, make decisions, and scale.

- **Now:** MBA candidate, UC Berkeley Haas '27
- **Before:** Six years consulting, Deloitte & KPMG
- **Focus:** Operations & transformation
- **Drawn to:** Infrastructure-intensive and complex environments

Before business school, I spent six years in consulting with Deloitte and KPMG, helping organizations redesign operations across payments, banking, telecommunications, manufacturing, energy, and the public sector. My work ranged from improving transaction operations for digital payments platforms to redesigning procurement processes, strengthening operating models, and leading transformation initiatives across multiple markets.

Across those experiences, I realized I was most energized by one type of problem: helping organizations untangle complexity. Whether the challenge was operational inefficiency, fragmented decision-making, or scaling across functions, I enjoyed understanding how a system worked, identifying where it broke down, and designing practical ways to improve it.

Today, I'm exploring how that work changes in the age of AI. Rather than thinking about AI as another technology to adopt, I'm interested in how it reshapes operating models, decision-making, and execution. I'm particularly curious about the organizations building and operating critical infrastructure, from AI infrastructure and energy systems to payments, digital identity, and other industries where reliability, scale, and operational excellence matter.

At UC Berkeley Haas, I'm building on that foundation through coursework in operations, strategy, and AI, while working on projects that combine market strategy, operational thinking, and AI-enabled workflows. Most recently I worked with an AI hardware startup on its supply chain operating model, and advised a digital identity and payments company on its European market-entry strategy, using AI to accelerate research while keeping business judgment at the center of the work.

Long term, I hope to help organizations navigate large-scale operational change, combining operations strategy, AI transformation, and cross-functional execution to build businesses that are more resilient, more efficient, and positioned for lasting competitive advantage.
