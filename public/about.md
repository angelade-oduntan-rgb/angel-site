# About — Angel Ade-Oduntan

## Hi, I'm Angel.

I've always been fascinated by how complex systems work. Whether it's an organization, a city, or a piece of technology, I'm naturally curious about how the different parts come together, where they break down, and how they can work better.

That curiosity led me to six years in consulting with Deloitte and KPMG, where I helped organizations redesign operations across payments, banking, telecommunications, manufacturing, energy, and the public sector. My work ranged from improving transaction operations for digital payments platforms to redesigning procurement processes, strengthening operating models, and leading transformation initiatives across multiple African markets.

Over time, I realized I was most energized by one type of problem: helping organizations untangle complexity. Whether the challenge was operational inefficiency, fragmented decision-making, or scaling across functions, I enjoyed understanding how a system worked, identifying where it broke down, and designing practical ways to improve it.

Today, I'm exploring how that work changes in the age of AI. Rather than seeing AI as another technology to adopt, I'm interested in how it reshapes operating models, decision-making, and execution. I'm especially curious about organizations building and operating critical infrastructure—from AI infrastructure and energy systems to payments and digital identity—where reliability, scale, and operational excellence matter.

At UC Berkeley Haas, I'm building on that foundation through coursework in operations, strategy, and AI while applying those ideas to real-world consulting and product strategy projects. During my International Business Development project, I advised a multinational identity and payments company on its European market-entry strategy, using AI to accelerate research while keeping business judgment at the center of the work.

Long term, I hope to help organizations navigate large-scale operational change by combining operations strategy, AI transformation, and cross-functional execution to build businesses that can adapt, scale, and operate better in an increasingly AI-driven world.

## Outside Work

The same curiosity that shapes my professional interests also shapes how I spend my time outside work. I'm happiest exploring—whether that's wandering through a new city, getting lost in an art museum, or painting abstract pieces at home. I'm fascinated by how cities work, from their architecture and public spaces to the cultures, transportation systems, and history that shape everyday life, which explains my habit of watching documentaries and YouTube deep dives on places around the world.

Travel is one of my favorite ways to learn. Long before moving to the United States, I'd already explored roughly three-quarters of Nigeria, my home country. Since then, work has taken me across six African cities, along with New York and Paris. Paris changed how I travel, reminding me that the best discoveries often come without an itinerary. The Musée de l'Orangerie remains my favorite museum in the world, and standing before Monet's *Water Lilies* is still my favorite museum experience.

Creativity and movement keep me grounded. I enjoy abstract painting for the freedom of creating without a plan, and I stay active through Pilates, cycling, tennis, and badminton—the racket sport I actually play most whenever I can find a court.
